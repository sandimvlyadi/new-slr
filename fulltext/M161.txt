                                                                                           Due to remaining challenges in generalizability and real-
                                                                                       world deployment, several directions remain for future
                                                                                       exploration. Improving the neighbor retrieval process through
                                                                                       attention mechanisms or domain-aware similarity measures
                                                                                       could enhance the quality of contextual embeddings and lead to
                                                                                       more discriminative features. Expanding the evaluation of the
                                                                                       framework to other medical imaging tasks, such as rare disease
                                                                                       detection or multi-label classification, would help assess its
                                                                                       versatility. Incorporating temporal or longitudinal patient data
                                                                                       may also enable dynamic and personalized predictions,
                                                                                       especially in progressive conditions. Furthermore, adapting the
                                                                                       framework for lightweight deployment on edge or mobile
                                                                                       devices could facilitate real-time clinical inference, supporting
                                                                                       broader integration into healthcare workflows. These
                                                                                       enhancements will contribute to advancing retrieval-augmented
                                                                                       learning for medical image classification.
