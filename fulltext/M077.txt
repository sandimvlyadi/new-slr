                                                                                                               V. D ISCUSSION

                                                                                     This study presents a method that leverages both labeled
                                                                                  and unlabeled knee X-ray images to achieve a fully automatic
                                                                                  OA diagnosis. The experiments on Dataset 1 demonstrate that
                                                                                  our detection model can localize knee joints accurately with a
                                                                                  mean IOU of 0.89 and an SSL algorithm leveraging unlabeled
                                                                                  X-ray images can improve accuracy by 8% on KL grade
                                                                                  classiﬁcation. Hence, in practice, our proposed algorithm can
                                                                                  automatically leverage raw plain radiographs to improve knee
                                                                                  OA diagnosis while signiﬁcantly reducing the costs of labeling
                                                                                  data.
