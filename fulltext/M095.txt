  Abstract: CORA-Net is a calibration-first, robust, and ordinally consistent system for grading knee osteoarthritis (OA)
  (Kellgren–Lawrence; KL-0…KL-4) from radiographs. It addresses four deployment hurdles—ordinal violations, class
  imbalance, label noise, and inter-site distribution shift. The model couples an EfficientNet-B5 backbone and feature
  pyramid network (FPN) with a dual-path cross-scale module: deformable spatial attention targets joint-space
  narrowing and osteophytes, while a channel-gated token mixer stabilizes subtle texture patterns. Two predictors are
  trained jointly—a categorical softmax head and a cumulative ordinal regression (CORAL)-based ordinal head—linked
  by a consistency penalty that enforces monotonic grade ordering. Learning is shaped by a class-balanced Focal–
  Tversky loss, uncertainty-guided co-teaching to suppress mislabeled examples, stochastic weight averaging with
  exponential moving average (SWA-EMA) self-distillation for stable convergence, and a Dirichlet calibration term. At
  inference, temperature scaling (fit on a held-out calibration split), lightweight test-time batch-norm adaptation, and
  conformal prediction provide calibrated probabilities and coverage-controlled risk sets. Evaluation uses strict patient-
  level stratified 5×2 cross-validation plus an external multi-center cohort, reporting macro-F1, per-class precision–
  recall AUC (PR-AUC)/ROC AUC (AUC), Cohen’s κ, expected calibration error (ECE), Brier score, conformal
  coverage/size, confidence intervals, ablations, and paired tests. Across internal cross-validation and external multi-site
  testing (e.g., Site-A: 860 radiographs; Site-B: 845 radiographs), CORA-Net Ultra achieves Macro-F1 = 0.982, Macro
  PR-AUC = 0.995, and ECE = 0.009 (Brier = 0.028), with 95% BCa bootstrap confidence intervals (1,000 resamples)
  and Holm–Bonferroni–corrected paired tests, improving minority-grade sensitivity and reducing calibration error
  compared with strong CNN/Transformer baselines.
  Keywords: Knee osteoarthritis (OA), Kellgren–lawrence grading, Radiograph-based grading, Ordinal classification
  (CORAL), Dual-path cross-scale attention, Deformable spatial attention, Channel-gated token mixing, Uncertainty-
  guided co-teaching, Class-imbalance learning, Calibration (ECE, Brier).
